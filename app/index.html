Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no difficulty setting, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Around 200 hours in total, most of it after the story.

    Missable trophies

    Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story. Cheats switch trophies off for the session, so keep them off on a trophy run.
